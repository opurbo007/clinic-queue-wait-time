How it works

  1. Each doctor has a "next free at" time, in minutes from now. A free doctor is 0. A doctor who is mid-consultation is free after the average time minus the minutes already spent, never below 0.
  2. The patients ahead of you who are not yet in a room go to the doctor who becomes free first. That doctor's free time then moves forward by their average consultation time.
  3. Repeat for every patient ahead. When none are left, your wait is the earliest free time among all doctors.

It works for any number of doctors and any queue position. The cost is O(n x d) for n patients ahead and d doctors. A min-heap would make it O(n log d).

Assumptions: nobody has a doctor preference, and each doctor's average time is used for every patient. A consultation that runs past its average counts as "about to finish", so the estimate is a lower bound.
